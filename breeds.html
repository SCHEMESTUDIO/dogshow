The dog breeds, with notes

We have been watching dogs on a glowing screen since 2026 began, which gives us a particular perspective on breed. Other guides will tell you what a Bernedoodle is. We can tell you what a Bernedoodle does at the moment it realises a camera is on. Both are useful, in their way. These are our notes.
