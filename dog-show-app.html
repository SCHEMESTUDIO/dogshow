dogshowapp.com: A Different Kind of Tool

Dog Show App is an Android phone app for dog show handlers, with a download for the Android phone app or a web app you can use on any device, letting you set up a personalized dog show schedule. It puts estimated ring times, judge names, ring numbers, and entry numbers at your fingertips.

That's a scheduling tool for people physically attending an AKC show, not an entertainment platform. If you don't have an Android device, there's a web-based app you can use on a tablet, computer, iPhone, or anything else. You can try the app free for 30 days.

If you landed here searching "dogshowapp" hoping to track an upcoming show, that's the product to look for. If you just want something to watch on a break, keep reading below.

Competition-Management Apps, Compared

A few named tools come up often in this space, each solving a slightly different problem for exhibitors actively campaigning a dog through a season.

ShowPoints

ShowPoints brings a dog show calendar, judging schedules, ring time alerts, and title progress together in one app. It builds a virtual ribbon rack featuring traditional AKC placement colors and lets you estimate potential AKC championship points using breed, division, and entry information. Results are entered by users, and ShowPoints calculates progress using applicable published rules. Official points and awards are determined by AKC.

Pricing runs on a subscription: ShowPoints Pro is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year, and the app is independent, not affiliated with or endorsed by the American Kennel Club or other event organizations.

Bucket Bitch

Bucket Bitch helps conformation exhibitors plan shows, record results, track dog progress, understand judge history, save show photos, manage handlers, and prepare for the ring with confidence. It leans more toward a personal database than a points engine: Bucket Bitch is upfront that it is not a show points calculator.

It runs on a freemium model, with a free tier covering the basics and a paid tier adding event entry, results logging, and cloud sync.

dogshowapp.com

Covered above. It's the lightest-weight of the three, focused purely on ring-time scheduling rather than points, judge history, or media.

How to Pick One

Match the tool to what you're actually doing:

  • Watching dogs for fun, no stakes involved: The Dog Show. Open it in a browser and go.
  • Attending a real AKC show and need ring times at a glance: dogshowapp.com's Android or web app.
  • Tracking championship points, majors, and a full season across shows: ShowPoints.
  • Keeping a long-term record of judges, results, and handler notes: Bucket Bitch.

There isn't a single best choice across all of these, because they're solving different problems for different people at different moments of a show weekend.
